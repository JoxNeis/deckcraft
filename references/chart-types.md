| #   | Chart                          | When to use it                                                                                          |
| --- | ------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 01  | Column, latest bar highlighted | A single metric over time, especially to point at the latest value or growth rate (CAGR)                |
| 02  | Ranked horizontal bar          | Ranking categories, such as market share by player; good for long labels                                |
| 03  | Line with direct labels        | Trends of several series over time, with one series to emphasise                                        |
| 04  | 100% stacked column            | How a mix or share changes over time when the total doesn't matter                                      |
| 05  | Waterfall (bridge)             | Explaining how you get from value A to value B, like a profit bridge or variance walk                   |
| 06  | Bubble matrix                  | Comparing items on two metrics plus size, such as a portfolio or prioritisation view                    |
| 07  | Dumbbell                       | Before vs. after for many categories, focused on the gap                                                |
| 08  | Diverging stacked bar          | Survey answers on an agree–disagree (Likert) scale, positive vs. negative                               |
| 09  | Clustered column               | Comparing 2–3 series across a few categories side by side                                               |
| 10  | Stacked column with totals     | Both the total and its breakdown over time                                                              |
| 11  | Marimekko (mekko)              | Market structure: segment size (width) and player share inside each segment (height)                    |
| 12  | Slope                          | Change between exactly two points in time, and who overtook whom                                        |
| 13  | Stacked area                   | How the composition of a growing total evolves, such as an energy or revenue mix                        |
| 14  | Donut                          | Share of total with 5 or fewer parts, plus a headline number in the middle                              |
| 15  | Bullet                         | KPI actuals vs. targets, with performance bands; a compact dashboard view                               |
| 16  | Pareto                         | The 80/20 rule: which few causes drive most of the problem                                              |
| 17  | Tornado                        | Sensitivity analysis: which assumptions move the result most                                            |
| 18  | Gantt / roadmap                | Project timelines, workstreams, and milestones                                                          |
| 19  | Heatmap                        | Spotting patterns or hot spots across two categorical dimensions, like region × sector                  |
| 20  | Small multiples                | Many series where one combined chart would be cluttered; same scale, one panel each                     |
| 21  | Scatter with trend line        | Showing a correlation between two variables and pointing out outliers                                   |
| 22  | Histogram                      | Distribution of one variable: skew, long tail, where the median sits                                    |
| 23  | Box plot                       | Comparing spread and variability across groups                                                          |
| 24  | Range bar                      | Minimum–maximum ranges with a typical value, such as pay bands or price ranges                          |
| 25  | Lollipop                       | A lighter-looking ranked bar for many items, often with a benchmark line                                |
| 26  | Combo (dual axis)              | Two related metrics in different units, like revenue (bars) and margin (line)                           |
| 27  | Cost curve                     | Options ranked by cost per unit, with width showing how much each delivers (abatement or supply curves) |
| 28  | Variance bar                   | Actual vs. budget or plan by unit: who's over and who's under                                           |
| 29  | Harvey balls matrix            | Qualitative scoring of options against criteria, such as strategic choices or vendor selection          |
| 30  | Funnel                         | Conversion through sequential stages, to find where the biggest drop-off is                             |

A rule of thumb for choosing:

- **Change over time:** 01, 03, 12, 13
- **Composition:** 04, 10, 11, 14
- **Ranking or comparison:** 02, 09, 25
- **Explaining a difference:** 05, 17, 28
- **Distribution:** 22, 23, 24
- **Relationship between metrics:** 06, 21
- **Decisions:** 29, 27, 18
